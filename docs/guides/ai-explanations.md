# AI Explanations

The optional Explain buttons, written by Anthropic's API from the calculated figures. Back to the [README](../../README.md).

## AI Explanations

**AI Explanations** (PRD §6.9) are off unless `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` is set in `.env` ([Configure](getting-started.md#configure)), as [ADR 0005](../../docs/architectural-decision-record/0005-ai-explains-calculated-scores-opt-in.md) decides. Without it `GET /api/explain` answers `{ "enabled": false }`, no Explain button is shown anywhere, `POST /api/explain` answers `404`, and nothing calls Anthropic.

With it, an **Explain** button is under the figures it explains: at the foot of the Location Overview (**Explain the Location Overview**), inside each Category Score's breakdown (**Explain the Safety score**), and in the Land panel for the Land Opportunity chosen (**Explain this Land Opportunity**). It opens the questions it offers first (PRD FR-11.3), then a box for a question of your own of up to 500 characters. The Overview's questions are the Scout Mode's own question first ("Could I open a café here?", "Should I buy a home here to live in?"), any it adds ("Is this area good for families?" for Homebuyer and Business: Nursery), then "What are the biggest risks here?" and "What stands out most in these figures?"; a Category Score's ask why it is what it is, which input counts most, and how much it counts in the Location Score; a Land Opportunity's why it warrants investigation, what to check before pursuing it, and what the land sales and Planning Applications near it suggest. They are config, in `EXPLAIN_QUESTIONS` (`apps/api/src/explain/questions.ts`), so a Scout Mode added by config asks its own question with no change there. `GET /api/explain` gives them all at once, with the model, so the page asks once and a new Scout Mode loads nothing more.

The answer streams in as it is written, under **"AI Explanation, based only on the figures above"** (FR-11.4), then says roughly how many tokens it took, "About 3,412 tokens: 3,170 sent and 242 written.", as Anthropic counted them (FR-11.5). A new Search Area, Scout Mode or set of weights puts it away, as it was about the figures before.

**What the model is sent** (FR-11.2) is built by the backend, not the browser, from the same Data Source calls as the page's own requests, which the cache answers, so explaining calls no source again (`apps/api/src/explain/facts.ts`). It is the structured figures already shown and nothing else, as JSON, then the question:

- **the Overview:** the Search Area, its local geography and the Scout Mode; the Highlights; the Location Score as it is shown, weighed by your own weights if you chose some, with each category's weight, share and points and those left out; every Category Score with its breakdown; and each Layer's status and statistics, the Planning Layer's Planning Applications too.
- **a Category Score:** the same Search Area and Scout Mode, its breakdown, the Location Score, and the statistics of the Layers it is calculated from.
- **a Land Opportunity:** its tags, area, road distance, INSPIRE coverage, whether it is Possibly Unregistered, its indicative Ownership Category with the titles matched around it and the note on how they are matched, its Planning Constraints and flood zone, its estimated capacity, how many INSPIRE Index Polygons overlap it, the land sales within 1 mile of it, labelled "indicative: may not be land", the Planning Applications within 500 m among the Search Area's, and FR-5.7's wording.

Features, outlines, ids and coordinates are left out (but the Location's): they are drawn on the map, not figures to explain. NG17 1AA's Overview is about 20,000 characters, some 5,000 to 6,000 tokens, a Category Score's 6,000 to 8,000 characters, and Sutton Parkway's Land Opportunity's, with its 76 land sales, about 14,000. The model is told, in instructions that hold no digit of their own (`apps/api/src/explain/prompt.ts`), to use only those figures and never introduce a number that isn't in them, no averages, estimates or sums of its own; to write every number in digits; to explain the scores from their breakdowns and never change them or score anything itself; to say when a Layer or score is unavailable, not prepared or waiting for its provider's turn; and to use Location Scout's terms. Where the figures include a Land Opportunity, the Land Opportunity asked about or any in the Overview's Search Area, it is also told a Land Opportunity is never available, for sale, unowned or free, that Possibly Unregistered land still has an owner, and to repeat FR-5.7's wording, "Our data suggests this parcel warrants further investigation…", whenever it mentions one. The scores are never the model's: they are calculated before it is asked, by the rules in [Scores](scores-highlights-and-scout-modes.md#scores).

The model is `ANTHROPIC_MODEL`, Claude Sonnet 5.5 (`claude-sonnet-5-5`) unless set, asked through Anthropic's own SDK (`@anthropic-ai/sdk`) with streaming, low effort and at most 4,096 tokens (`apps/api/src/explain/model.ts`). The SDK is given Anthropic's address, the key and the app's `fetch`, so it reads nothing else from the environment, such as `ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL`. A model that doesn't take `output_config.effort`, such as Claude Haiku 4.5, is refused, and the panel says to check `ANTHROPIC_MODEL`. `POST /api/explain` answers as server-sent events, each one's `data` an `ExplainEvent`: the text as it is written, then `done`, with its tokens, or `error`, saying why: Anthropic refused the key, the model, or its rate limit, didn't answer, or the model declined. Each finished answer is kept in memory for 24 hours, keyed by the model and everything it was sent, so the same question about the same figures, in the same Search Area, Scout Mode and weights, is answered again at once, without asking Anthropic, and says so ("Given again from memory."); figures that have changed since, such as Planning Applications that were queued, are asked about afresh. Like every cache it is never written to disk, and a restart empties it.

Each question asked afresh is one request to Anthropic with your key: an Overview explanation is about 5,500 tokens in and 300 out.

**Numbers the figures don't have** are a defect (FR-11.6). The backend checks every answer as it finishes (`checkNumbers` in `apps/api/src/explain/number-check.ts`): each number written in digits must be one it was sent, in its instructions, figures or question, as given ("2,024" or "2024"), rounded to as many places as the answer gives it (223.6 as 224), or a share as a percentage (0.727 as 72.7%). A difference, total or average of the model's own fails, as does any number from elsewhere. The panel flags any it finds: "Check this answer: 987,654 is not among the figures above, and an AI Explanation should use no other." Numbers written in words can't be checked, which is why the model is told to use digits.

`pnpm test:ai` is the **opt-in AI check** (NFR-3.5): it asks your `ANTHROPIC_MODEL`, with your `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` from `.env`, every question offered about recorded inputs, the figures the fixture-backed API makes from its recordings (`AI_CHECK_CASES` in `apps/api/src/explain/ai-check.ts`): NG17 1AA's Overview in the General, Homebuyer and Business: Café Scout Modes, its Safety score in General and its Schools score in Homebuyer, and Sutton Parkway's farm field, a Land Opportunity, in Development, 19 questions in all. Only the model is called; every Data Source answers from its recording. It checks each answer against exactly what the model was sent, and fails, naming them, if any has a number that isn't in it, or an answer about a Land Opportunity leaves out FR-5.7's wording, printing each answer that failed and the tokens used: its 19 prompts are about 280,000 characters, roughly 75,000 tokens in, and some 6,000 out. Without a key it says what to set, asks nothing, and fails. It isn't part of `pnpm test`, which never reaches the network: `ai-check.test.ts` runs the same check against the stubbed model, which passes it, makes up a number that fails it, and leaves out the wording, which fails it too.
